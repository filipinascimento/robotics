Robotics Atlas

A visual atlas of robotics research.

Robotics Atlas presents the structure of the robotics literature through two complementary perspectives: a temporal view of citation communities and a spatial view of the institutional landscape behind the field. Together they help situate major themes, track how they evolve, and inspect where robotics research is produced.
